# Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how {productName} ("{productName}", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use {productName} as a Chrome extension and/or self-hosted web application.

By using {productName}, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

- The {productName} browser extension
- The {productName} self-hosted web application
- Related documentation and support interactions

It covers data processing performed by or on behalf of {productName} in connection with these services.

## 2. Data We Process

Depending on how you use {productName}, we may process:

- Account data: email address, profile identifier, authentication provider identifier (for cloud-authenticated extension mode)
- Job tracking data you enter or import: company, role, status, salary information, location, interview notes, links, and related metadata
- Settings and preferences: language, theme, dashboard settings, privacy preferences, retention preferences
- Technical data: app logs needed for reliability and security (limited and purpose-bound)

## 3. Data Sources

We collect data from:

- You, when you enter data manually
- Browser interactions, when you trigger extension features
- Optional connected providers (for example OAuth identity providers), only for authentication and account linkage

## 4. Why We Process Data

We process data to:

- Provide core job tracking functionality
- Sync your data across devices when cloud sync is enabled
- Secure accounts and prevent abuse
- Improve product reliability and fix defects
- Comply with legal obligations

## 5. Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

When required by applicable law, we process personal data under one or more of these legal bases:

- Performance of a contract (providing the service you request)
- Legitimate interests (service security, quality, and fraud prevention)
- Consent (where explicitly requested)
- Legal obligation (where required by law)

## 6. Storage Modes and Retention

{productName} supports different deployment modes:

- Self-hosted mode: your application data is stored in your own environment (for example local SQLite via your deployment).
- Extension cloud mode: your application data is stored in configured cloud infrastructure for sync and account access.

Retention depends on your usage and settings. You can export your data and request deletion as described below.

We do not sell personal data.
