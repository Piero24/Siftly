# Analytics & Reports

Siftly provides rich analytics to help you understand and optimize your job search.

## Available Charts

### Application Timeline

Line chart showing applications submitted over time. Helps identify your activity patterns and search intensity.

### Status Funnel

Conversion funnel from Applied → Interviewing → Offer. Shows your success rate at each stage.

### Response Rate

Percentage of applications that received any response vs. no response. Helps evaluate which strategies work best.

### Work Type Distribution

Pie/donut chart breaking down remote, hybrid, and onsite applications.

### Employment Types

Distribution across permanent, intern, and fixed-term positions.

### Salary Distribution

Histogram of salary ranges to understand your target market.

### Company Charts

Bar chart of most-applied companies and sectors.

### World Map

Geographic visualization showing where your applications are distributed globally. Click a country to filter.

### CV Profiles

Usage distribution across your different CV versions.

## Time Range Filtering

All charts respect the time range selector:

- **Today** — applications submitted today
- **7d** — last 7 days
- **30d** — last 30 days
- **1y** — last year
- **Total** — all time

## Data Accuracy

Analytics are computed in real-time from your application data. There is no separate analytics database — charts always reflect the current state.
